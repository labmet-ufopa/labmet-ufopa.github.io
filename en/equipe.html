Faculty

Laboratory lecturers

  • Julio Tota da Silva

    Coordinator · Atmospheric Sciences

    Meteorologist trained at UFPA, with an MSc in Meteorology from Inpe and a PhD in Climate and Environment from Inpa. He works on atmospheric physics and chemistry and on the exchange of carbon, water and energy between the forest and the air.

    He is the local coordinator of CarbonARA-Brazil and deputy coordinator of the radiosonde tracking project.

  • Helvecio Bezerra Leal Neto

    Professor · Artificial Intelligence

    PhD and MSc in Applied Computing from Inpe and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Ufopa. He works on artificial intelligence and data science applied to environmental monitoring, focusing on automatic storm tracking and very short-range rainfall forecasting.

Students

Come and work with us

The laboratory welcomes undergraduate students for undergraduate research, internships and final-year projects. The work involves installing and maintaining instruments, fieldwork at the Ufopa Experimental Farm and the Tapajós National Forest, programming and data analysis.

Anyone interested can contact one of the laboratory's lecturers or the Atmospheric Sciences course coordinators.
